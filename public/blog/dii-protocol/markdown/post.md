Most decisions get judged by how they turn out. The DII Protocol asks a different question first: *did this decision come from who I actually am?*

## What Is the DII Protocol?

The DII Protocol — the **Decision Integrity Index** — sits at the heart of [The Resonance Core Framework™](/resonance-core-framework). It's an index that shows, objectively, how well your decisions match who you're becoming.

That word *objectively* matters. Our memory is finite, and our minds are very good at telling flattering stories about the choices we've made. The DII replaces "I think that went well" with real data — so you can see where you're aligned, and where you're quietly drifting.

## Why It Starts With Journaling

The DII runs on consistent, honest data, and the first source of that data is your journal. Written consistently and objectively, a journal becomes a bank of information that shows *trends* in how you make decisions — the raw, unfiltered record the DII uses to score and benchmark your alignment.

When it applies, each entry captures:

- Emotional and somatic cues
- Narrative themes and perceived judgments
- The event(s) that occurred
- How aligned you felt with your identity
- Any reframing or integration you did
- Whether the decision felt resonant or dissonant
- Outcomes and patterns you notice
- A DII score for each decision

Without that record, there's no frame of reference — and decisions made without one are easily washed away by the mind's preconceived notions.

## Scoring on a Simple 1–5 Scale

Words like "good," "fine," or "OK" mean something different to everyone, so they make poor markers. The DII uses numbers instead.

Score each key decision from **1 to 5**:

- **4–5** — resonant decisions that feel expansive and aligned
- **1–3** — dissonant decisions that feel contractive or misaligned

A "4" tells you clearly that you performed well. A "2" tells you something wasn't fully aligned and is worth a closer look. Over time, those numbers become trends — an honest picture of your alignment across weeks and months.

## Scores Are Personal Markers, Not Achievements

This part matters most. DII scores are **never** a measure of success, self-worth, effort, or competition. We're trained from childhood to treat numbers as grades, but that black-and-white thinking has no place here.

A DII score is for you alone — like your journal — to show where you're going and how you got there. It's data, not a verdict.

## The Six Core Values

Every DII score is anchored to the framework's six core values:

- **Embodied Awareness** — decisions grounded in presence and self-awareness
- **Modular Precision** — a life structured into clear, adaptable parts that plug into a coherent core
- **Resilient Alignment** — staying connected to who you are in real conditions, not just ideal ones
- **Somatic Discipline** — reading and regulating your cues so you respond rather than react
- **Creative Truth** — expressing your reality without needing others to confirm it
- **Strategic Innovation** — applying your values and DRIVEs to create new, aligned solutions

## The Decision Alignment Score (DAS)

As the protocol deepens, individual scores roll up into the **Decision Alignment Score** — a single view of how aligned a decision is. It draws on three sub-scores:

- **SSI (Self-State Integrity)** — how aware you were of your own alignment or misalignment in the moment you decided: whether you were acting from who you are now or from outdated patterns
- **EROI (Emotional Return on Investment)** — whether the physical and emotional effort you put into something leads to outcomes that feel worthwhile, aligned, and sustainable
- **Shadow Index** — the pull of shadow (contractive) cues on a decision, which lowers the overall score when fear, avoidance, or distortion was doing the deciding

## Start Today

You don't need the whole protocol to begin. Journal one decision today, give it a score from 1 to 5, and write one line on why. Do that for a week and you'll already see patterns you couldn't see before.
