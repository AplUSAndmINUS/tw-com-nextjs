## Decisions

- **Spec 4.2:** "on the home page" read as this page, since that is where
  the role cards and stats grid are.
- **Pre-order elements:** they follow `RCF_PREORDERS_OPEN` (#273). That
  covers the quick link, the book badge and button, and the author fact.
- **Instagram:** `InstagramGrid` keeps the zip's pool and backfill. The new
  `layout='feature'` renders one lead tile and four squares. The server
  render is a skeleton in the same shape. No /instagram folder was in
  either zip; the open-source origin (instagram/embed-instagram-feed, MIT)
  is noted in the component and its logic is already ported.
- **Faker:** added with `yarn add -D`; the zip's yarn.lock was not copied.
  Mock mode only runs in DEV/TEST builds, and a postbuild check guards
  PROD.
