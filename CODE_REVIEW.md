# Code Review: `/about-me` landing page and related changes

**Reviewer role:** independent PR review, against the owner's stated requirements only
**Date:** 2026-11-14
**Branch state reviewed:** uncommitted working tree on top of `baseline`
**Mode:** findings only. At the owner's request, **no fixes are applied**; each finding has a recommendation for the owner to action.

---

## Verdict

**Approve with changes.** 9 of 12 requirements are fully met, 2 are partly met (5 and 11), and 1 is met with a minor gap (8). No high-severity defects: nothing user-visible is broken, and the build, typecheck and all tests pass.

**13 findings** (5 Medium, 8 Low), all open. **10 observations** are noted for the owner's judgement.

---

## Method

- Read the full diff (`git diff` plus the new files) against the 12 requirements.
- **Content provenance:** extracted every copy string from `about-me/page.tsx` and `aboutMeData.ts` and searched the **baseline commit** (all of `src/`, `public/blog`, `public/portfolio`) for each sentence.
- Ran the typecheck, both test suites and the production build, and checked the sticky hero in the browser against the static export (8 viewport sizes, 360x740 to 1023x1366).
- Each recommended fix was prototyped and shown to work during the review (including a test for each that fails when the fix is undone), then **reverted** at the owner's request. The recommendations below are therefore known to be viable.

## Observations (owner decision)

- **O1. UI microcopy written for this page (Req. 5).** These strings are not in the repo. They are headings, labels and prompts rather than claims about the owner, and the owner approved the page visually, but they should be signed off explicitly:
  - Headings and kickers: "In 60 seconds", "Three roles, one system", "What I'm building", "The book and the podcast", "Talent bars", "What I bring to the table", "Selected projects", "Some of the work", "From the blog", "Recent writing", "Find me online", "Listen on", "Core concepts".
  - Skills lede: "The skills behind the three roles, scored out of 100."
  - Closing band: "That was the short version" / "Want to go deeper?" / "Read the full story, start a conversation, or dig into the framework behind all of it."
  - Buttons and links: "Read my full story", "Explore the framework →", "Browse episodes →", "About the show", "See the portfolio", "Consulting on Fluxline.pro".
  - Role titles and labels: "20+ years building platforms" (from the `/about` stat), "Host, The Resonant Identity", "Pre-orders open".
  - SEO title and description ("Terence Waters in 60 Seconds | …").
  - "Podcaster · Systems Architect · Business Consultant" is the owner's own wording from the brief.
- **O2. Podcast card spacing.** If F3 is applied (one verbatim sentence), the podcast card, which is the same height as the book card, gains empty space above its buttons on desktop. Options: accept; add the role card's verbatim sentence; or let the cards size independently.
- **O3. Role chips** abbreviate `/about` skill groups (e.g. "Azure & M365" from "Azure Cloud & M365").
- **O4. Arrow glyphs in button labels** ("Explore the framework →") are read aloud by screen readers. This follows the home page's convention, but `ArrowLink` on the same page hides its arrows with `aria-hidden`. Consider aligning site-wide.
- **O5. Raw pixel values.** `about-me/page.module.scss` uses about 55 literal px values, consistent with `/about`'s stylesheet; several map to `--tw-space-*` tokens. Worth a site-wide token pass rather than a one-page change.
- **O6. Scroll handler reads.** `TwHeroScrollReveal` calls `getComputedStyle` on the hero's children each animation frame to find the pinned photo. Reads come before the single write (no layout thrash), and no jank was measured; caching the photo and its `top` on resize would trim the work.
- **O7. Podcast art weight.** `ResonantIdentity_icon.png` (271 KB, 1024px) is shown at 96px. It is lazy-loaded and below the fold, but a 192px asset would save about 260 KB.
- **O8. Safari/iOS not verified in a real engine.** The sandbox could not download WebKit. Sticky positioning and `overflow: clip` need Safari/iOS 16 or later; on older iOS the photo won't pin but still fades. Recommend a check on a real iPhone.
- **O9. Pre-existing, out of scope:**
  - the home nav overflows by 17px at 820px (owner is tracking this);
  - Vite's "ESM syntax in a file loaded as CommonJS (vitest.config.ts)" notice;
  - two long lines in `TwHero.tsx` that fail Prettier at baseline;
  - every CRLF file fails Prettier's default `endOfLine: lf` (`"endOfLine": "crlf"` in `.prettierrc` would fix this repo-wide).
- **O10. Process notes.**
  - `api/newsletterRateLimit.test.js` was changed in an earlier round, outside the requested scope (since accepted by the owner).
  - `jsdom` and `@types/jsdom` were added without prior sign-off (since accepted; future dependency changes will be raised first).
  - This review changed no code. Fixes were prototyped and then reverted at the owner's request.
